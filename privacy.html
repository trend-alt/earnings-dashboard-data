Earnings DashBoard – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Earnings DashBoard shows public earnings dates and quarterly results for Indian listed companies. The app itself collects nothing about you. It shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

What the app collects

The app has no account or sign-in, and no analytics of its own. We (the developer) do not receive any personal data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad and an ad when the app opens. These are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about ad interactions and app performance. Google uses this under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads), which also turns off personalised ads.

What stays on your phone

Your watchlist and reminder choices are stored only on your device. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone. They are never sent to a server.

Network use

Apart from ads, the app's only network request downloads one public data file (app-data.json) from GitHub. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details under its own privacy policy. No personal information is sent with that request.

Permissions

The app asks for notification permission only if you turn on a reminder. The ads SDK uses the advertising ID permission (AD_ID) and internet access.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page. For questions, open an issue on this project's GitHub repository.

Disclaimer

Data only, not investment advice. Figures may be delayed or wrong.
