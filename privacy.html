Earnings DashBoard – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Earnings DashBoard shows public earnings dates and quarterly results for Indian listed companies. It is built to collect nothing about you.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account or sign-in, and the app has no ads, analytics, tracking or crash reporting that sends data anywhere.

What stays on your phone

Your watchlist and reminder choices are stored only on your device. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone. They are never sent to a server.

Network use

The app's only network request downloads one public data file (app-data.json) from GitHub. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details under its own privacy policy. The app sends no personal information with that request.

Permissions

The app asks for notification permission only if you turn on a reminder.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page. For questions, open an issue on this project's GitHub repository.

Disclaimer

Data only, not investment advice. Figures may be delayed or wrong.
